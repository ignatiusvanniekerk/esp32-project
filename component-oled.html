SSD1306 OLED

0.96" I2C display — 4 wires: VCC, GND, SDA, SCL · shares the I2C bus with the VL53L0X ToF sensor

What it is. A 128×64 monochrome OLED on a 4-pin I2C module. Inside the housing it sits behind the 28 × 18 mm window in the front panel. Four wires only — it is the very first module you should wire and test.

3. Sketch pins

Normal DevKit

#define SDA_PIN 21
#define SCL_PIN 22

Adafruit_SSD1306 display(128, 64, &Wire, -1);

ESP32 Mini / C3

#define SDA_PIN 8
#define SCL_PIN 9

Adafruit_SSD1306 display(128, 64, &Wire, -1);
Watch out.
  • Power the OLED from 3V3, not 5V — 5V will damage most modules.
  • Some 4-pin modules are labelled SCL/SDA the other way round. If the screen stays black, swap the two signal wires.
  • OLED and ToF live on the same SDA/SCL pair — you only daisy-chain them, do not run two sets of wires to different pins.
Test first. Flash the OLED-only sketch before adding anything else. If the panel lights up you have confirmed 3V3, ground, and both I2C lines all the way to the display.
